Magic Wand Cleaning questions and answers

What areas do you service?

Magic Wand Cleaning serves homes in Lethbridge, Alberta. For move-out cleaning, surrounding areas may be considered depending on location, timing, and job size.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as access is arranged ahead of time.

What do I need to do before you arrive?

We recommend picking up personal items and sharing priority areas before service begins.

Do you supply cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. We bring our own cleaning products and equipment.

Do you offer eco-friendly or pet-safe products?

No. Eco-friendly and pet-safe product options are not currently available.

Do you offer move-in and move-out cleaning?

Yes. Move-in and move-out cleaning is available to help you start fresh or leave a space spotless.

How can I book cleaning?

Call or text +1 587-377-0572, use email at [MISSING_BUSINESS_EMAIL], or send a message through the approved Magic Wand Cleaning Facebook page.

What is your pricing?

Pricing is simple and hourly. Magic Wand Cleaning charges $30 per hour, with a 3-hour minimum per visit.

Do you have set business hours?

No set business hours are posted. Weekend and emergency availability may be offered based on scheduling.

Do you offer commercial or office cleaning?

No. The website currently advertises residential cleaning services only.
