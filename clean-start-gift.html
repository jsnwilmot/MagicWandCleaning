Giving Back

The Clean Start Gift

Magic Wand Cleaning believes a clean home can make life feel lighter.

A monthly cleaning donation for our community

Each month, we donate up to 4 hours of cleaning to someone in Lethbridge or the surrounding area who could use a helping hand.

You can nominate a senior, caregiver, new parent, single parent, community volunteer, neighbour, friend, or family member. The goal is simple: give someone a clean start when they need it most.

What’s Included

The Clean Start Gift includes up to 4 hours of standard residential cleaning.

This may include general cleaning such as kitchens, bathrooms, floors, dusting, wiping surfaces, and other regular home cleaning tasks.

Important Details

  • One cleaning gift is selected each month.
  • The nominee must be located within the Magic Wand Cleaning service area.
  • The cleaning gift is based on availability.
  • This program is for standard residential cleaning only.
  • Hazardous cleaning, biohazards, hoarding situations, pest-related cleaning, or unsafe work environments are not included.
  • Submitting a nomination does not guarantee selection.
  • Recipients are contacted privately. Names, photos, or stories are only shared with permission.
