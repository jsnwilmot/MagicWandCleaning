About Us

Local cleaning with a personal touch

Magic Wand Cleaning is a local, owner-operated business based in Lethbridge, Alberta with 20+ years of cleaning experience.

A clean space makes everything feel better.

We take pride in the details so you can enjoy more of what matters most. From recurring cleaning to one-time deep cleans, we have got you covered.

Magic Wand Cleaning is mostly solo-operated, with occasional help from a daughter when needed. Our goal is simple: dependable, high-quality residential cleaning with honest communication and a personal touch.
