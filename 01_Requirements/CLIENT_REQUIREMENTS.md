## Business or department

Magic Wand Cleaning, a local cleaning business serving Lethbridge, Alberta and nearby areas.

## Problem statement

Magic Wand Cleaning needs a clear online presence that helps local customers quickly understand what services are offered, where the business operates, and how to request cleaning services. Without a strong website, potential customers may rely only on Facebook, word of mouth, or scattered information, which can make the business harder to find and harder to trust.

## User needs

- Target users:
- Local homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers, and small business owners in Lethbridge and nearby areas who need reliable cleaning services and want an easy way to learn about Magic Wand Cleaning, view services, and request a quote.

## Required features

- Required features:
- The initial website must include the following approved capabilities:
- 1. Homepage
- A clear homepage that introduces Magic Wand Cleaning, explains the business, highlights the main cleaning services, and guides visitors toward requesting a quote or contacting the business.
- 2. Services section
- A service section that lists the cleaning services offered, such as residential cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, and general cleaning support.
- 3. Local service area content
- Content that clearly identifies Magic Wand Cleaning as a local Lethbridge cleaning business and supports searches from Lethbridge and nearby areas.
- 4. Contact options
- Clear contact options, including phone, email, Facebook link, contact form, or quote request link, depending on the final contact method approved by the business owner.
- 5. Calls to action
- Visible calls to action that help visitors request a quote, ask a question, or contact Magic Wand Cleaning from key areas of the website.
- 6. Branding
- Use of the approved Magic Wand Cleaning logo, colours, favicon, visual style, and business branding.
- 7. Mobile-first responsive layout
- A layout that works properly on mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
- 8. Before-and-after or proof-of-work section
- A section for cleaning photos, before-and-after images, reviews, testimonials, or other proof of work if available.
- 9. SEO setup
- Basic SEO setup, including page titles, meta descriptions, local keywords, heading structure, image alt text, Open Graph metadata, and clean page content.
- 10. Footer
- A footer with business contact details, service area, navigation links, and a small Rose & Paw Digital Designs credit link.
- 11. Privacy policy
- A basic privacy policy page if the website includes a contact form, analytics, or any collection of visitor information.
- 12. Performance and accessibility basics
- Fast-loading pages, optimized images, readable text, accessible buttons, clear headings, good colour contrast, and labelled forms.

## Workflows

- Workflows:
- 1. Visitor learns about the business
- Trigger: A visitor lands on the website from Google, Facebook, referral, or direct link.
- Steps: Visitor opens the homepage, reads the business introduction, reviews services, checks the service area, and looks for proof of work or trust content.
- Outcome: Visitor understands who Magic Wand Cleaning is, what services are offered, and whether the business serves their area.
- 2. Visitor requests service or a quote
- Trigger: A visitor selects a contact or quote call to action.
- Steps: Visitor chooses phone, email, Facebook, or contact form. If a form is used, the visitor enters required details and submits the request.
- Outcome: Magic Wand Cleaning receives the request and can follow up with the potential customer.
- 3. Visitor checks cleaning services
- Trigger: A visitor wants to know what cleaning work is available.
- Steps: Visitor reviews the services section or services page, reads short service descriptions, and selects a contact option if interested.
- Outcome: Visitor can decide whether the service fits their need.
- 4. Visitor checks service area
- Trigger: A visitor wants to know if Magic Wand Cleaning works in their location.
- Steps: Visitor reads the service area section showing Lethbridge and approved nearby areas.
- Outcome: Visitor confirms whether they are likely within the service area.
- 5. Visitor views proof of work
- Trigger: A visitor wants confidence before contacting the business.
- Steps: Visitor views before-and-after photos, testimonials, reviews, or other trust content if available.
- Outcome: Visitor gains confidence in the quality and professionalism of the business.
- 6. Contact form submission
- Trigger: A visitor submits the contact or quote request form.
- Steps: The form validates required fields, checks basic spam protection if enabled, and sends the message to the approved business email.
- Outcome: The business receives the inquiry. The visitor sees a success message or clear next step.
- 7. Content update request
- Trigger: Magic Wand Cleaning requests a website update.
- Steps: The business owner provides the change request, Rose & Paw Digital Designs updates the website files, tests the change, and publishes the update.
- Outcome: The website reflects the approved change.
- 8. Website deployment
- Trigger: Website files are updated and pushed to the approved repository.
- Steps: The hosting platform builds and publishes the updated static website. Rose & Paw Digital Designs checks the live website after deployment.
- Outcome: The updated website is live.
- 9. SEO discovery workflow
- Trigger: A person searches online for cleaning services in Lethbridge or nearby areas.
- Steps: Search engines read the website content, metadata, headings, service area wording, and structured information where available.
- Outcome: The website can appear in relevant local search results and bring visitors to the site.
- 10. Analytics review, if enabled
- Trigger: Analytics or Search Console are connected and collect website activity.
- Steps: Rose & Paw Digital Designs or the business owner reviews traffic, search terms, popular pages, and contact activity.
- Outcome: Future website improvements can be based on visitor behaviour and search performance.

## Security requirements

- Permission rules:

The first version of the Magic Wand Cleaning website will not include user accounts, customer login, staff login, or role-based access controls.

Public website visitors can only view public website content and use approved contact options. They cannot edit website content, access private information, view submissions, or manage website settings.

Magic Wand Cleaning can review content, approve business information, request updates, approve photos, confirm services, and approve launch decisions. The business owner will not directly edit the website through an admin dashboard in the first version.

Rose & Paw Digital Designs can build, edit, publish, update, and maintain the website. This includes managing website files, hosting setup, SEO settings, contact form setup, image optimization, deployment, and technical fixes.

If a contact form is used, form submission access should be limited to the approved business email and website administrator as needed for setup and troubleshooting.

Hosting, repository, DNS, analytics, and form service access should only be granted to authorized admin users.
- Sensitive data notes:

The first version of the website should collect as little personal information as possible.

Sensitive or personal data may include:

1. Contact form details
   Customer name, email address, phone number, requested service, message, and preferred contact method.

2. Customer inquiry details
   Information a visitor provides about their home, business, cleaning needs, schedule, or location.

3. Analytics data
   Basic website activity data if analytics are enabled, such as page visits, device type, referral source, and general location data.

4. Business account access
   Hosting, DNS, repository, analytics, and form service login details are sensitive and must only be accessed by authorized administrators.

Handling expectations:

Personal information should only be used to respond to customer inquiries or provide requested cleaning service information.

The website should not publicly display customer contact details, private messages, addresses, or inquiry information.

Contact form submissions should only be sent to the approved Magic Wand Cleaning email address and accessed by authorized users.

The website should avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Address details should only be requested if needed for quoting or service follow-up.

If a contact form or analytics are used, the website should include a privacy policy explaining what information may be collected and how it is used.

Passwords, API keys, form service keys, analytics credentials, hosting credentials, and DNS credentials must not be stored in public website files or shared publicly.

## Branding notes

- Branding notes:
- The website should use the approved Magic Wand Cleaning logo, favicon, colours, and visual style from the provided brand assets and business card.
- The visual design should feel clean, friendly, professional, local, and trustworthy. It should avoid looking like a generic template. The design should support a small local Lethbridge cleaning business while still feeling polished and modern.
- The website should use soft cleaning-themed colours, strong contrast, readable fonts, rounded or friendly design elements, and bright imagery where appropriate. The fairy or magic wand theme can be used lightly as a brand detail, but it should not make the website feel childish or overly decorative.
- The voice should be simple, warm, clear, and confident. Content should explain services in plain language and help visitors quickly understand what Magic Wand Cleaning offers, where they work, and how to contact them.
- The website should sound local and approachable while still presenting the business as reliable, professional, and detail-focused.

## Automations

- Automations:
- The first version of the website will have limited automation because it is planned as a static business website.
- Approved automations may include:
- 1. Contact form submission
- When a visitor submits the contact or quote request form, the message is sent automatically to the approved Magic Wand Cleaning email address.
- 2. Form validation
- Required form fields are checked before submission so incomplete messages are not sent.
- 3. Spam protection
- Basic spam protection may be added to reduce unwanted form submissions.
- 4. Deployment automation
- When website updates are pushed to the approved repository, the hosting platform can automatically rebuild and publish the website.
- 5. SEO file generation
- The website build process may automatically generate or update technical files such as sitemap.xml where supported.
- 6. Analytics tracking
- If approved, basic analytics can automatically collect website traffic data such as page views, referral sources, and device type.
- 7. Email link and phone link actions
- Phone and email links open the visitor’s phone or email app automatically when selected.
- No advanced automation, CRM workflows, online booking automation, payment processing, customer accounts, or recurring service reminders are included in the first version.

## Constraints

Some service details, final copy, photos, and contact preferences may need to be confirmed before launch. The site should be simple to maintain, fast to load, SEO-ready, and suitable for affordable static hosting such as Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages.
