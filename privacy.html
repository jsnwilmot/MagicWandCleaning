Privacy policy

How your contact information is used.

This policy explains how Magic Wand Cleaning handles the information you choose to share when requesting a quote or making contact.

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Magic Wand Cleaning respects your privacy. This website is intended to collect only the information reasonably needed to respond to cleaning questions and quote requests.

Information you may provide

When you request a quote or contact Magic Wand Cleaning, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, preferred contact method, general location, requested service, and details about your cleaning needs.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to respond to your inquiry, discuss the requested cleaning service, prepare a quote, and arrange service when agreed upon. It is not intended to be sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Website quote form

The website quote form is currently a static demonstration and is not connected to an online form delivery provider. If a provider is added later, this policy should be updated to identify that provider and explain how it processes submissions.

Facebook and phone contact

If you contact Magic Wand Cleaning through Facebook or by phone, those services may handle information according to their own privacy practices.

Information retention and security

Contact details should be kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide requested service, maintain necessary business records, or meet legal obligations. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask about the personal information you have shared or request that it be corrected or deleted, subject to reasonable business and legal recordkeeping needs.

Contact

For privacy questions, call 587-377-0572 or contact Magic Wand Cleaning through its Facebook page.
