Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of information handling for this Phase 2 static website.

Last updated: June 29, 2026

This policy describes the current Phase 2 website. It is provided for general information and is not a claim of legal completeness.

Information collected through this website

This is a static informational website. It does not include an online contact form, customer account system, booking system, payment system, or customer database.

Magic Wand Cleaning may receive information that you choose to share through direct contact by phone or through Facebook. That information may include your name, contact details, location, requested service, cleaning priorities, and message.

How direct-contact information may be used

Information shared directly may be used to respond to questions, discuss cleaning needs, prepare a quote, arrange follow-up, or provide requested service information.

Payments and accounts

This website does not collect online payments and does not create visitor or customer accounts.

Analytics and tracking

No analytics or tracking service is approved or included in this Phase 2 website.

Third-party contact

If you contact Magic Wand Cleaning through Facebook, your use of that service is also subject to Facebook's own terms and privacy practices.

Data sharing and retention

Magic Wand Cleaning should use direct-contact information only for responding to inquiries and providing requested cleaning-service information. No automated website database stores customer inquiries in Phase 2.

Your choices

You can limit the personal information you share when making an inquiry. Please do not send sensitive payment credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information through direct contact.
