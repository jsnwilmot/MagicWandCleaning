## Screen purpose

- Feature descriptions:
- 1. Homepage
- The homepage introduces Magic Wand Cleaning, explains that it is a local Lethbridge cleaning business, highlights the main services, builds trust, and directs visitors to contact the business or request a quote.
- 2. Services section
- The services section lists the cleaning services offered in clear language. Each service should briefly explain what the customer can expect and who the service is best suited for.
- 3. Local service area content
- The website clearly states that Magic Wand Cleaning serves Lethbridge and nearby areas. The content should support local search visibility and help visitors confirm that the business serves their location.
- 4. Contact options
- Visitors can easily find ways to contact Magic Wand Cleaning, such as phone, email, Facebook, or a contact form. Contact options should be visible from key areas of the website.
- 5. Calls to action
- The website uses clear buttons and links that guide visitors to request a quote, ask a question, or contact the business. Calls to action should appear near the top of the page, after service sections, and in the footer.
- 6. Branding
- The website uses the approved Magic Wand Cleaning logo, colours, favicon, and visual style. Branding should feel consistent across the full website.
- 7. Mobile-first responsive layout
- The website is designed for mobile users first and adjusts properly for tablet and desktop screens. Text, buttons, images, and sections should remain easy to use on all screen sizes.
- 8. Proof-of-work section
- The website includes before-and-after photos, testimonials, review highlights, or other trust content where available. This section helps visitors see the quality of the cleaning work before contacting the business.
- 9. SEO setup
- Each page includes basic SEO elements such as page titles, meta descriptions, headings, local keywords, image alt text, and Open Graph metadata. Content should support searches for cleaning services in Lethbridge.
- 10. Footer
- The footer includes business contact details, service area, key navigation links, and a small Rose & Paw Digital Designs credit link.
- 11. Privacy policy
- If the website collects visitor information through a contact form or analytics, it includes a basic privacy policy that explains what information may be collected and how it is used.
- 12. Performance and accessibility
- The website loads quickly, uses optimized images, readable text, clear headings, accessible buttons, strong colour contrast, and properly labelled forms.

## Navigation notes

- Workflows:
- 1. Visitor learns about the business
- Trigger: A visitor lands on the website from Google, Facebook, referral, or direct link.
- Steps: Visitor opens the homepage, reads the business introduction, reviews services, checks the service area, and looks for proof of work or trust content.
- Outcome: Visitor understands who Magic Wand Cleaning is, what services are offered, and whether the business serves their area.
- 2. Visitor requests service or a quote
- Trigger: A visitor selects a contact or quote call to action.
- Steps: Visitor chooses phone, email, Facebook, or contact form. If a form is used, the visitor enters required details and submits the request.
- Outcome: Magic Wand Cleaning receives the request and can follow up with the potential customer.
- 3. Visitor checks cleaning services
- Trigger: A visitor wants to know what cleaning work is available.
- Steps: Visitor reviews the services section or services page, reads short service descriptions, and selects a contact option if interested.
- Outcome: Visitor can decide whether the service fits their need.
- 4. Visitor checks service area
- Trigger: A visitor wants to know if Magic Wand Cleaning works in their location.
- Steps: Visitor reads the service area section showing Lethbridge and approved nearby areas.
- Outcome: Visitor confirms whether they are likely within the service area.
- 5. Visitor views proof of work
- Trigger: A visitor wants confidence before contacting the business.
- Steps: Visitor views before-and-after photos, testimonials, reviews, or other trust content if available.
- Outcome: Visitor gains confidence in the quality and professionalism of the business.
- 6. Contact form submission
- Trigger: A visitor submits the contact or quote request form.
- Steps: The form validates required fields, checks basic spam protection if enabled, and sends the message to the approved business email.
- Outcome: The business receives the inquiry. The visitor sees a success message or clear next step.
- 7. Content update request
- Trigger: Magic Wand Cleaning requests a website update.
- Steps: The business owner provides the change request, Rose & Paw Digital Designs updates the website files, tests the change, and publishes the update.
- Outcome: The website reflects the approved change.
- 8. Website deployment
- Trigger: Website files are updated and pushed to the approved repository.
- Steps: The hosting platform builds and publishes the updated static website. Rose & Paw Digital Designs checks the live website after deployment.
- Outcome: The updated website is live.
- 9. SEO discovery workflow
- Trigger: A person searches online for cleaning services in Lethbridge or nearby areas.
- Steps: Search engines read the website content, metadata, headings, service area wording, and structured information where available.
- Outcome: The website can appear in relevant local search results and bring visitors to the site.
- 10. Analytics review, if enabled
- Trigger: Analytics or Search Console are connected and collect website activity.
- Steps: Rose & Paw Digital Designs or the business owner reviews traffic, search terms, popular pages, and contact activity.
- Outcome: Future website improvements can be based on visitor behaviour and search performance.
